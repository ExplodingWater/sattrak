## 3. Simulation limits

Sattrak is intended for exploration, education and experimentation. Its
spacecraft motion, attitude, visibility, atmospheric-entry effects and CFD
results are simulations or predictions. Catalog refreshes do not make orbital
predictions live spacecraft telemetry. Data can be old, incomplete or incorrect;
models and numerical methods have limitations documented in the project.

Sattrak is not represented as certified flight software, a navigation service,
an operational tracking authority, or a safety-critical engineering tool.
Independently validate information before relying on it for an operational,
scientific or engineering decision. This describes intended use and limitations;
it is not an additional restriction on your MIT license rights.

## 4. Local files and optional network services

Sattrak can operate using bundled or saved data. If you enable internet updates,
the application can contact CelesTrak and SatNOGS to retrieve satellite and
ground-station information. Those services control their own availability,
data and applicable terms. They receive the network information needed to
handle requests, such as your IP address. Updates may fail or use older saved
data. You can change the update preference in Settings.

Your imported files, local preferences and saved recordings remain subject to
your own file management. Save recordings you want to keep and maintain your
own backups. Unsaved work can be lost when you start another recording or exit;
recovery features are not a guarantee against data loss.
