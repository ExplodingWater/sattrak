# Planned background music

This is the user's requested list, in supplied order. Only the six agreed 2003 Toyota Corolla recordings are currently bundled.

## Playback rules

- Music is optional.
- Start 45–60 seconds after opening ag.exe.
- Fade in the first song.
- Always fade in Joakuim's **End of the winter**, including when reached by skipping or restarting, regardless of playlist position. Fade duration is not yet specified.
- Top player bar: restart/previous, pause/resume, end/next. Seek slider with elapsed and remaining time.
- The Kane Parsons track is tentative; exclude it from the default playlist until confirmed.

## Preliminary rights/source check

Permission to use the six listed 2003 Toyota Corolla tracks in sattrak was received directly from Haylee by email. Haylee stated that the tracks contain extremely transformed samples derived from Nintendo system music and authorized their use while sattrak remains under the MIT license. That permission covers Haylee's rights; Nintendo has not separately cleared the underlying samples. Preserve the original email privately as the permission record. No permission to bundle the other listed recordings has been established.

- C418: [official Volume Alpha release](https://c418.bandcamp.com/album/minecraft-volume-alpha) lists all rights reserved.
- Lloyd Vaan: [Present](https://lloydvaan.bandcamp.com/track/present) and [Hello, world](https://lloydvaan.bandcamp.com/track/hello-world) list all rights reserved. [Lens Flare](https://music.amazon.in/albums/B09HV1R9XL) includes Watching planes take off; [Peony / Kernel Hope](https://music.amazon.com/albums/B0BZZMCQFY) identifies the release. No app redistribution permission found.
- Cave Wave: [composer's release](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p6IVID1WagQ) identifies Stunshine and David Anderson Kirk. No app redistribution permission found.
- Joakuim: [Flow LP listing](https://volumo.com/album/31095-flow-lp) identifies the recording. Rights-holder permission still needs verification.
- Kane Parsons: [official distributed album listing](https://open.spotify.com/album/3pC0SguPN8JNkHfCnjgEFb) credits copyright and recording rights to Kane Parsons. No app redistribution permission found.
- 2003 Toyota Corolla: artist permission received for the six tracks above. Keep the recordings outside the MIT grant and credit Haylee / 2003 Toyota Corolla.

The built-in player also accepts local files in `assets/music`. The six agreed 2003 Toyota Corolla MP3s are installed there in chronological order. The 2003 and 2004 tracks came from the Internet Archive release linked by [Haylee's official album page](https://haylee.garden/albums/2003toyotacorolla.html); the remaining four came from her official hanahata YouTube uploads and were converted to MP3 for the player. Their credits and permission summary are in `assets/music/CREDITS.md`, with source URLs and checksums in `assets/music/sources.json`. Other requested recordings are not bundled.
