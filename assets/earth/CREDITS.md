# Earth surface texture

`earth_texture.jpg` in the repository root is NASA Earth Observatory's
Blue Marble: Next Generation, June 2004 global composite with topography.

- Source: NASA Earth Observatory.
- [Dataset and downloads](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography/)
- [Original JPEG](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography/june/world.topo.200406.3x5400x2700.jpg)
- Downloaded September 14, 2026, unchanged except for the filename.
- Dimensions: 5400 × 2700 pixels, north-up equirectangular projection.
- SHA-256: `2fa9dc8314e463073199c79b6716fa92e25248d158f67344ff04fa8016bd5f23`

This is a static seasonal composite with shaded land relief, not live imagery
or geometric terrain. NASA describes this download as approximately 8 km per
pixel; close surface views therefore cannot show street-level detail.
The renderer supplies its own lighting and atmosphere.

NASA imagery is credited separately from the project's MIT-licensed code.
See [NASA media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).
