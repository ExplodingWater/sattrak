Converted with scripts/bake_spacecraft.py: static node transforms, centering, material grouping, base-color textures and roughness. Source meter scale retained. Rendering uses approximate lighting and alpha cutouts, without normal/metallic maps or full glTF PBR. See conversion.json for geometry bounds and source checksum. Selecting the model does not change simulation mass, inertia or orbit.
